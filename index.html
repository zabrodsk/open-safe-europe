A few useful answers

Before the
first signature.

Is this one SAFE for all of Europe?

No. The app serves founders across Europe. Its bundled agreement is a general template, with no national adaptation or legal review claimed. Selecting a country does not make that template compliant with local law. You can also upload a template of your own.

Can I send or sign an agreement here?

You can draft and edit a complete SAFE agreement, then download Word and PDF files. Signatures and payments happen outside this app.

Does a UK ASA qualify for SEIS?

Eligibility depends on the agreement and wider conditions. HMRC expects a non-refundable subscription, no interest, restrictions on variation, cancellation and assignment, and a longstop date. Its general expectation for advance assurance is no more than six months. Read HMRC's guidance.

What if my country has no document listed?

You can use the general SAFE template, clearly labelled as not adapted to your country, or upload your own plain-text template. The app does not claim to resolve national legal requirements.

Where do my draft details go?

Drafts are saved in this browser on your device. You can export a backup or delete the saved draft. Optional AI sends the selected text and your instruction directly to OpenAI only when you choose to send it. Your API key stays in memory and is never saved with a draft. Fonts are hosted with the app. There is no analytics or account system.
